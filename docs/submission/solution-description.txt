BatchLens is an agentic quality investigator inside a playable factory. For the Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency challenge, Pip uses real Gemini on Vertex AI to read labels and inspection records, call validated evidence and trace tools, compare possible causes, and propose inspection missions. Operators approve missions, enter labelled synthetic results, and ask Gemini to reassess its findings with source citations. Case-specific operational chat clarifies evidence and refuses general queries. Human approvals govern accepted relationships, action drafts and inspection handovers. Cloud Run hosts the React/Three.js and FastAPI app; a named Firestore database persists cases, results, review history and frozen HTML/CSV exports. Cloud Build and Artifact Registry deliver the container. Firebase Hosting and Authentication are not used. The Northstar case and inspection results are synthetic; no production accuracy or savings are claimed. Team: PixelTrendAnalytics.
